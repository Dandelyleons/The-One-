Good To Know

Leasing details

Reservation requirements

Reserving a unit requires a ₱5,000 deposit, plus 2 months' deposit, 2 months' advance, photocopies of two valid IDs with photo, and complete post-dated cheques.

What's not included in the rate

Rates are exclusive of water, electricity, parking and wifi. The admin office can help you set up a parking space and wifi installation.

Security deposit

The security deposit is returned 60 days after your move-out date, once management has received final utility bills to process the refund against.

Roommates

The team can assist with endorsing a roommate, but for residents' safety and security, The One does not match strangers as roommates.
