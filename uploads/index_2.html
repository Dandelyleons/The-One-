Frequently Asked

Common questions about The One properties

Which The One property is closest to UST?

The One Santo Tomas has a direct footbridge to the University of Santo Tomas. The One Richville Place and The One Padre Campa are also within the UST area.

Is there a The One property near De La Salle University?

The One Dasma Place is located near University De La Salle Medical Center and De La Salle College of Medicine in Dasmariñas, Cavite.

What unit types are available across the four properties?

Two main unit types: Bare (kitchen counter top, wardrobe, closet and basic bathroom fixtures) and Semi-Furnished (adds a bedframe, study table and chair). The One Dasma Place also offers a nightly Serviced Apartment. Use the Find Your Home tool above to filter by property, unit type and budget.

What's the difference between a Semi-Furnished and a Bare unit?

Semi-Furnished rooms include a bunk bed frame, study table with chair, clothes cabinet, kitchen countertop and basic bathroom fixtures. Bare rooms have bare wall finishes only. Prices vary by unit size and number of persons per room.

How do I reserve a unit?

Reserving a unit requires a ₱5,000 deposit, plus 2 months' deposit, 2 months' advance, photocopies of two valid IDs with photo, and complete post-dated cheques.

What's not included in the rate?

Rates are exclusive of water, electricity, parking and wifi. The admin office at each property can help you set up a parking space and wifi installation.

How does the security deposit work?

The security deposit is returned 60 days after your move-out date, once management has received final utility bills (e.g. Meralco) to process the refund against.

Can you help me find a roommate?

The team can assist with endorsing a roommate, but for residents' safety and security, The One does not match strangers as roommates — we recommend rooming with someone you already know.

How far is The One Dasma Place from the Manila properties?

The One Dasma Place is approximately 32 kilometers from the Sampaloc/España cluster via the Cavite–Manila route.
